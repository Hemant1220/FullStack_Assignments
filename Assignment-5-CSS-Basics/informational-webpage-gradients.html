JAPAN · TRAVEL GUIDE

Discover Kyoto

Historic streets, quiet gardens, and centuries of craft make Kyoto a memorable place to explore.

01 / CULTURE

Tradition in every detail

Kyoto is known for temples, shrines, traditional architecture, and arts such as tea ceremony and textile making. Walking through its older districts offers a glimpse of Japan's cultural history.

02 / NATURE

A quieter side of the city

Seasonal gardens, wooded paths, and the Arashiyama area invite visitors to slow down and notice the landscape. A radial gradient gives this section a soft, sunlit focal point.

Plan a thoughtful visit

Check opening hours, respect local customs, and allow time to explore beyond the busiest streets. Spring and autumn are popular seasons, so planning ahead can help.
