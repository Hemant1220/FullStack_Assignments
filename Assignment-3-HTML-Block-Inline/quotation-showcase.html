Words Worth Remembering

A small collection of quotations about learning and life.

Taking the First Step

The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.

Lao Tzu

Learning by Doing

Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn.

Benjamin Franklin (commonly attributed)

Knowledge and Wonder

The important thing is not to stop questioning. Curiosity has its own reason for existing.

Albert Einstein (commonly quoted)

Why Quotes Inspire Us

Quotations can summarize a useful idea in a few memorable words. They may encourage people to keep learning, reconsider a challenge, or take the first step toward a goal. Their meaning is strongest when we connect the words with our own actions.
